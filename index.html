Course Overview

AI-assisted Software Engineering is a hands-on course on building software with coding agents. It moves through three stages: first the basis — how AI and large language models work, and how they support software engineering; then the techniques — the basic methods of applying AI across the engineering lifecycle; and finally the application — a real-world team project where you practice using AI to build, test, and secure a working product.

Goals

Course Goals

The term is built in three stages. Each one depends on the one before it: you cannot apply what you have not practiced, and you cannot practice what you do not understand.

1. Basis

Learn how AI/LLM work and how they support software engineering

2. Techniques

Learn the basic methods of using AI in software engineering

3. Application

Do a real-world project to practice the usage of AI in software engineering

Course Schedule

Fourteen weeks, meeting Tuesdays and Thursdays. Eleven teaching weeks move from language models to agents, prompting, requirements, coding practice, and teamwork, then the hard parts — testing, security, repair, and operations — closing with guest speakers. The last three weeks are the project competition and demonstrations. Each session links its lecture slides (.pptx) alongside the assigned readings.
