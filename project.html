Course Project

Teams of three will use AI coding agents and a spec-driven workflow to build a locally runnable, full-stack recruiting and candidate-matching platform. Development runs through Week 10, followed by a Week 11 feature freeze, cross-team auditing and repair in Week 12, and final presentations in Weeks 13–14; together, requirements fulfillment, peer review, and the presentation make up 60% of the overall course grade.
